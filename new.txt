On October 28, an Israeli soldier was killed when Palestinian terrorists attacked Israeli troops in the southern Gaza Strip, despite there being a ceasefire. But for CBC News, and correspondent Chris Brown, the real threat to the ceasefire was not the actions of Palestinian terrorists in actually breaking it, but with Israel, for daring to strike back, targeting Hamas operatives inside the coastal enclave.

In an October 28 report for the CBC News program The National, Brown spoke with host Adrienne Arsenault, and in a segment, “Israel tests ceasefire with ‘powerful attacks’ in Gaza,” Brown told her that “these strikes, these Israeli strikes…are probably the biggest test yet for the ceasefire.”
